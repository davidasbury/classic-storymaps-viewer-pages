HTTP and browser observations independently confirmed its missing-page response
and same-origin-only framing policy. The general implementation kept a direct
link available instead of attempting to bypass these restrictions.

check operated it successfully. Waiting for network idle before interaction made
both isolated Map Tour cases and the full 18-case run pass; production sandbox
permissions were not weakened to address a test-readiness problem.

## Learnings

- The shared startup gate provides cross-viewer error behavior without editing
  each legacy runtime bundle.
- An iframe load event does not reliably distinguish a usable cross-origin page
  from a framing refusal or remote error. Keep direct opening available.

## Notes for Future Me

The fallback requires a known, validated item source URL. It does not invent a
replacement website, fetch pages through a proxy, recover deleted stories or
bypass CSP, X-Frame-Options, authentication or browser mixed-content restrictions.
Scripts, forms and popups are allowed inside the frame; same-origin access and
top-level navigation are not. More demanding sites may require direct opening.
