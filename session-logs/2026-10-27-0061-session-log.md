## Learnings

- ArcGIS search totals describe candidates, not the gallery's filtered stories.
  Completed enumeration and incomplete progress need distinct labels.
- Metadata counting and visible-page rendering should be separate: completing
  a count must not move the page or reload its thumbnails.
