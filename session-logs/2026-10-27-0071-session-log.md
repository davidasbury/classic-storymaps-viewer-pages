### Problems Encountered

**Cross-origin failures were ambiguous.** An iframe cannot reliably distinguish
404 from blocked embedding. CORS-readable HEAD responses supplied the evidence;
timeouts, CORS failures and authentication errors did not authorize replacement.

**The archive still depended on legacy services.** Its document loaded, but the
map showed "Loading maps from ArcGIS Online" at inspection. The change repaired
the destination, not the archived application's external dependencies.

## Learnings

- A verified archive page is not evidence that its complete legacy map works.
- Browser HTML parsing checks must inspect resource requests and execution,
  not only serialized URL replacements.
