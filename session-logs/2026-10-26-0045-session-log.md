## Learnings

- Browser consent and approved-origin configuration are separate controls.
  An approved public hostname alone cannot prove every path is non-sensitive.
- Archive indexing and replay availability differ; successful metadata lookup
  does not establish that the image decodes or that recovery works for every photo.
