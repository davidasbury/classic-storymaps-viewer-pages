## Learnings

- Loaded layers and DOM-present gallery entries do not establish that the map
  is visible. Verify the foreground map and inspect a settled screenshot.
- An ArcGIS HTTP-200 response can contain an access-denied error. Neither that
  error nor an anonymous failure establishes that the item was deleted.
